How to Choose a Whole House Water Filter

When selecting a whole house water filter, consider these key factors:

  • Water source: City water vs. well water requires different filtration approaches
  • Contaminants: Get your water tested to know what you need to remove
  • Flow rate: Ensure the system can handle your household's peak demand
  • Filter life: Consider the long-term cost of replacement filters
  • Certifications: Look for NSF/ANSI certifications for verified performance
