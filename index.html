Why Water Filtration Matters in 2026

Municipal water supplies and private wells can contain contaminants including chlorine, lead, PFAS, and sediment. A quality water filter removes these impurities, improving taste, odor, and safety for your family.

Quick Comparison: Filter Types

Whole House: Best coverage, higher cost, professional install may be needed.
Under Sink: High performance, moderate cost, semi-permanent install.
Countertop: Easy setup, moderate performance, portable.
Pitcher: Lowest cost, slowest flow, no installation.
